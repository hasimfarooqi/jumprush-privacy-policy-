Privacy Policy — Jump Rush

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Jump Rush ("the Game") is an offline mobile game developed under the Vexlumo name. This Privacy Policy explains how the Game handles information when you use it.

2. Information Collection

Jump Rush is designed to work offline. Based on the current design, the Game does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play.

The Game does not display advertisements, according to its current design.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The Game may store gameplay information locally on your device, such as:

- Game progress and unlocked levels
- Coins, stars, and selected characters
- Game settings, including sound preferences

This information is used to save your progress and preferences. It is not intentionally transmitted to the developer by the Game.

Your device's Android backup settings may affect whether locally stored app data is included in a device backup.

4. Internet and Third-Party Services

The Game is intended to be playable offline. The developer does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information through the Game.

If future versions add online services, analytics, advertising, or other third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game does not intentionally request personal information from children. Since the Game is designed for offline play, users are not required to provide personal information to play.

6. Data Security and Deletion

Gameplay data stored locally can generally be removed by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game. This may permanently delete saved progress.

Data included in device backups may be subject to the device's backup settings and policies.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Game's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published at the same URL.
